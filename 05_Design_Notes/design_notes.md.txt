# Design Notes — Home Credit Loan Against Property Qualification Voice Agent

## 1. Project objective
Build a Retell AI voice agent that performs a preliminary eligibility check for Home Credit's Loan Against Property (LAP) offer of up to ₹75,00,000. The agent collects the required customer and property details, applies the stated eligibility rules, and determines whether the customer is qualified, disqualified, requires specialist transfer, or requests a callback.

## 2. Agent setup
- Platform: Retell AI
- Agent: Home Credit LAP Qualification Agent
- Conversation type: Single Prompt
- Voice: Kate
- Language setting: English (US)
- Greeting: “Hello, may I speak with {{customer_name}}?”
- The agent identifies itself as Ananya from Home Credit in the conversation.

## 3. Information collected
The agent should collect and extract the following fields when they are provided:
- `property_type`
- `ownership_status`
- `documents_available`
- `requested_loan_amount`
- `final_qualification_amount`
- `occupation`
- `income_mode`
- `market_value`
- `tenure_years`
- `qualification_status`

The agent should remember answers given out of order, use the customer's latest confirmed correction, clarify ambiguous answers, and avoid inventing information that was not supplied.

## 4. Business-rule decisions
- Maximum offer amount: ₹75,00,000.
- Eligible property types: residential, commercial, and industrial.
- Agricultural property: disqualify.
- Ownership may be sole or joint.
- Original property documents must be available; if unavailable, disqualify.
- Occupation must be salaried or self-employed.
- Bank-received income is eligible; cash income disqualifies.
- Eligible tenure: 3 through 15 years, inclusive.
- If the customer requests more than ₹75,00,000, explain the cap and ask whether they accept ₹75,00,000. If they refuse, disqualify. If they change their mind, confirm their revised acceptance before continuing.
- An existing loan on the property or an explicit request for EMI reduction requires specialist transfer, unless a disqualification condition has already taken priority.
- `QUALIFIED` is a preliminary eligibility outcome, not a guarantee of final loan approval.
- Exact rates and final next steps are provided by a senior loan expert; the agent must not invent rates or guarantee approval.

## 5. Qualification status definitions
- `QUALIFIED`: All required eligibility conditions are satisfied.
- `DISQUALIFIED`: At least one mandatory eligibility condition fails.
- `TRANSFER_REQUIRED`: The customer explicitly mentions an existing loan on the property or requests EMI reduction, and no disqualification rule takes priority.
- `CALLBACK_REQUESTED`: The customer requests a callback instead of continuing the current conversation.

A senior expert's routine follow-up after a successful preliminary check must not, by itself, cause the agent to set `TRANSFER_REQUIRED`.

## 6. Conversation design
1. Confirm that the agent is speaking to the intended customer.
2. Introduce the Home Credit LAP offer and explain that this is a preliminary check.
3. Collect property type and ownership.
4. Confirm availability of original property documents.
5. Collect requested loan amount and apply the ₹75,00,000 cap rule if needed.
6. Collect occupation and income mode.
7. Collect estimated property market value.
8. Collect requested tenure.
9. Apply the eligibility rules and communicate the appropriate outcome.
10. For specialist-transfer or callback scenarios, end the standard qualification flow appropriately.
11. Keep the conversation polite, concise, and clear.

## 7. Testing and validation approach
The agent was tested against 15 scenarios:
1. Happy path
2. Agricultural property
3. Original documents unavailable
4. Cash income
5. Requested amount exceeds cap; customer changes their mind
6. Existing loan on property
7. EMI reduction request
8. Answers provided out of order
9. Multiple details provided at once
10. Ambiguous document availability
11. Customer corrects an answer
12. Tenure below 3 years
13. Tenure above 15 years
14. Customer refuses the ₹75,00,000 cap
15. Customer requests a callback because they are busy

After prompt changes, selected scenarios were rerun to verify the qualification status and extraction behavior. Final reports should reflect the actual transcript and post-call extraction output rather than assumptions.

## 8. Known limitations and safeguards
- The agent performs a preliminary eligibility check only; it does not approve a loan.
- A callback or specialist follow-up should not be described as actually scheduled unless the connected workflow confirms that it has been scheduled.
- Values not discussed in a call should not be treated as customer-provided data.
- Qualification status must follow the business rules, not merely the fact that a senior expert will contact the customer.
- Post-call extraction should be checked separately from conversation behavior.

## 9. Expected outcome
The design aims to provide consistent rule-based qualification, accurate extraction of customer-provided information, correct disqualification and transfer routing, and clear communication about the next step.
