# Home Credit LAP Voice Agent — Test Cases

## Purpose

These test cases are designed to verify that the AI Voice Agent correctly follows the Home Credit LAP qualification rules while handling natural customer conversations.

The tests cover:

- Normal qualification
- Immediate disqualification
- Transfer scenarios
- ₹75 lakh limit
- Out-of-order information
- Ambiguous answers
- Customer corrections
- Busy customers
- Interruptions
- Multiple pieces of information in one response
- Missing information
- Customer objections

---

# Test Case 05 — Loan Amount Above ₹75 Lakh

## Expected Result

- Ask whether the customer would like to proceed with ₹75 lakh.

# Test Case 23 — Customer Gives an Unsupported Requirement

## Customer Says

"My property is worth ₹10 lakh. Am I definitely eligible?"

## Expected Result

Agent must NOT invent a minimum market-value threshold.

Agent should continue checking the actual specified eligibility criteria.

The agent must not claim eligibility or ineligibility solely because of the market value.

# Test Case 24 — Customer Refuses ₹75 Lakh Alternative

## Customer Says

"I need ₹90 lakh."

Agent:

"The maximum available under this offer is ₹75 lakh. Would you like to proceed with ₹75 lakh?"

Customer:

"No, I need at least ₹90 lakh."

## Expected Result

End the conversation politely.

Final status:

DISQUALIFIED

Reason:

Requested amount exceeds the maximum available amount for this offer and customer does not accept the maximum allowed amount.
