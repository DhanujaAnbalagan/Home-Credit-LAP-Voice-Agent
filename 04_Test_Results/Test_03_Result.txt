**Test objective:** Verify that the voice agent correctly disqualifies a customer who does not have the original property documents.

The customer confirms their identity, expresses interest in the Home Credit Loan Against Property offer, and provides the following details:

- Property type: Residential

**Customer response:** “No, I don't have the original property documents. The bank has them.”
